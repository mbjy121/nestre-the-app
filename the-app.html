Before you download

Questions, answered.

Can I use the app without a coach?

Yes. The app is built to guide you on its own — your daily practice, Frames, and Mindset Profile are all self-serve. A NeuroTrainer is there when you want more.

Is the training I do a class?

No — it's a personal practice. A few minutes a day, on your schedule, built around your own performance data rather than a fixed curriculum.

Does the app sync across my devices?

Your progress and Mindset Profile follow you across iOS and Android, so you can pick up wherever you are.

Where can I see my performance?

Everything lives in your profile — your NESTRE Score, history, and the Mindset Profile that reshapes as you train.
