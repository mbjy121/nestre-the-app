01 — Foundations

Color

A warm paper ground and a deep marine navy carry the brand; a single aqua accent does all the pointing. Text is near-black ink, never pure black. Aqua is a fill and highlight — for text on light grounds use Aqua Ink for contrast.

Usage ratio. Roughly 70% ground (paper/navy) · 25% ink & muted type · 5% aqua. The aqua earns its impact by being scarce — one accent per view, not scattered.

02 — Foundations

Typography

One family does everything: Instrument Sans, weights 400–700. Display sizes run large with tight negative tracking; labels run tiny, uppercase, wide. The tension between those two extremes is the type system.

Rule of thumb. The bigger the type, the tighter the tracking (down to −0.03em on display). The smaller the type, the wider — labels get +0.16em and uppercase. Never set body copy in aqua or uppercase.
